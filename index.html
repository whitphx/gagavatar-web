GAGAvatar web renderer

A made-up avatar, a sphere of Gaussians with a mouth on a neck and shoulders, is splatted on WebGPU at moving head vertices and shown without a refiner, its first three feature channels as colour. The head turns on the neck with the body kept still, or with the whole bust as GAGAvatar's camera turns it.
